# Hollow Meadow — The Trade
## Performance Script (emotion · inflection · pace)

Built from "Hollow Meadow — The Trade" (Google Doc, Sept 4 2026). Every line is kept word for word. Only direction was added.

---

### How to read the marks

| Mark | Meaning | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `[beat]` | a small breath between thoughts | about ½ second |
| `[pause]` | a real stop — a look, a thought landing | about 1 second |
| `[hold]` | let the moment sit; nobody talks | about 2 seconds |
| *CAPS* in a line | the word that gets the push | — |
| ↗ / ↘ | voice lifts / voice drops at the end of the line | — |
| `(action)` | what the body does while talking | — |

**Base pace:** about 2¼ words a second (roughly 135 words a minute). That is slower than normal kid-cartoon speed on purpose. Nobody steps on anyone else's line unless the direction says *overlap*.

**Rule for every shot:** a line ends, then the listener reacts on screen, *then* the next person talks. The reaction is part of the pace.

**Runtime warning:** the script has about 2,100 spoken words. At this pace, with the pauses, the episode lands near **18–20 minutes**. The "about 3 minutes" on the original was already off: even at normal cartoon speed, with no pauses, those words take about 11–12 minutes. If it needs to be shorter, cut lines or scenes, not pauses.

**Continuity note to decide (Scene 5):** Tavique says "What's on the hook—" as the flashlight goes into the box. But in Scene 2 he left the flashlight in the shed's junk box, not on the hook. Either the line changes, or Scene 2 needs a moment where it ends up back on the hook. Directed here as written.

## 1. The gift

*INT. KITCHEN — DAY. Afternoon light through the window. The old cabinet doors are sanded to bare wood, dust on the counter. A brass hook on the wall by the back door. The kitchen drawer to the left of the sink.*

*Mood: warm, easy, a little playful. This is the "before" — it should feel safe.*

**TAVIQUE** *(walking in, curious, bright — spots the bag)*
Hey Dad! ↗ [beat] What's that? [beat] You went to the store? ↗

**DAD** *(warm, teasing — pretends not to know)*
Hey Tavique! [beat] What's *what*? [beat] *(looks down at the bag, mock surprise)* Oh — *this?* [beat] Yeah. [beat] I needed to pick up some items to fix these old cabinets in the kitchen. ↘

**VESSA** *(leaning in from the table, wrinkling her nose)*
The ones that are all sanded and *dusty?* ↗

**DAD** *(chuckles, pats a cabinet door — a little puff of dust)*
Yep! [beat] *Those* ones. [pause] Let me show you what's in the bag.

*He sets the bag on the counter. The kids crowd in. Each item comes out slowly — a little show.*

**DAD** *(holding up a small box, rattles it)*
Screws. [beat] For the hinges.

**DAD** *(pulls out a tiny brush, holds it up like it's precious)*
A paintbrush.

**TAVIQUE** *(squinting, unimpressed, a small laugh)*
That's a *little* brush.

**DAD** *(patient, teaching — taps the corner of a cabinet with it)*
It's for the corners — [beat] so the paint can go all the way to the *edges*. [pause] *(sets down the brush, lifts a can)* And, lacquer. [beat] So the wood doesn't stay rough. ↘

**VESSA** *(peering into the bag, sly, pointing)*
There's something *else* in there. [beat] What's that? ↗

**DAD** *(slow reveal, a little proud — lifts it out with both hands)*
This? [beat] *THIS* is a brand-new flashlight. [beat] Still in the sleeve. [pause] For under the sink [beat] and the shed.

*The new flashlight gleams in its plastic. Tavique tilts his head.*

**TAVIQUE** *(genuinely puzzled, slow)*
But wait... [pause] How come we need *TWO* flashlights? ↗

**DAD** *(simple, gentle)*
We don't. [beat] I'm putting the new one in the drawer. *(opens the drawer, lays the new one inside)* [beat] Which means this old one… [pause] *(stops, remembers)* wait, let me get it.

*He reaches deeper into the drawer. Pulls out a scuffed metal flashlight. Holds it like it matters.*

**DAD** *(slower now, softer — this is the heart of the scene)*
This scuffed one right here. [beat] The one that's been in this drawer since before you could reach the counter. [pause] *(turns to Tavique)* That means this one belongs to *YOU* now.

**TAVIQUE** *(a little let down, not rude — honest kid disappointment)*
You're giving me the old *scratched* one? ↗

**DAD** *(kneels to Tavique's level, warm and certain — not scolding)*
I'm giving you the old and *faithful* one. [beat] The one that has *proven* itself. [pause] *(puts it in his hands)* Go ahead. [beat] Click it.

*CLICK. A strong beam hits the wall. Tavique's eyes widen.*

**TAVIQUE** *(surprised, won over, quieter)*
It works. [beat] It's *bright.* ↗

**DAD** *(smiling, remembering — each place gets its own little beat)*
It's bright, [beat] and it's *reliable.* [pause] I used it in the shed. [beat] I used it in that storm when the power went out. [beat] I used it under the sink when the pipe leaked. [pause] It *never* quits. ↘

**VESSA** *(nodding, a little wistful)*
That's the *good* one. [beat] I remember you using it.

**DAD** *(hand on Tavique's shoulder, earnest, slow)*
That's why I want *you* to have it, Tavique. [beat] Not the new one. [beat] *This* one. [pause] It's special because it already *proved* it. [pause] You take care of a light like that.

**TAVIQUE** *(sincere, a small proud smile, looking at the light)*
Okay. [beat] Thanks, Dad. [pause] *(looks to the wall)* I'll keep it on the hook.

**DAD** *(pleased, pointing at the brass hook by the back door)*
Good idea. [beat] Keep it on the *hook.* [beat] Not in a pile.

**TAVIQUE** *(walks over, hangs it carefully, says it like a promise)*
On the hook. ↘

*[hold] — the flashlight swinging gently on the brass hook. Nobody talks.*

## 2. Afterthought

*INT. HOUSE — OVER TIME. Three quick moments. Each one a little more careless. The music of the performance goes from careful → rushed → lazy.*

*— Tavique's bedroom, evening —*

**TAVIQUE** *(on his stomach, grumbling, peering under the bed)*
Aw man, [beat] I can't see under the bed. [beat] Where's that flashlight? [pause] *(looks up, remembers, relieved)* … There. [beat] On the hook. [beat] Got you.

*He fetches it from the hook, clicks it on, sweeps under the bed.*

**TAVIQUE** *(triumphant, then dutiful)*
There's my sock! ↗ [pause] Okay. [beat] Light goes back on the hook. [beat] *(hangs it, proud of himself)* Just like Dad said.

*LATER — hall closet*

**TAVIQUE** *(quicker now, distracted, digging)*
This closet's dark. [beat] I need that flashlight again. [pause] … There it is. [beat] And there's my cap. ↗

*He grabs the cap, drops the flashlight on the floor, turns to go.*

**VESSA** *(from the hallway, hands on hips, big-sister tone)*
Tavique? [beat] You left Dad's light on the *floor.* [beat] It goes on the hook. [pause] *Remember?* ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(defensive, fast, whiny)*
I was gonna put it back. [beat] I *was!*

**VESSA** *(flat, unimpressed, turning away)*
You *always* say that. ↘

*LATER STILL — the backyard shed, dim*

**TAVIQUE** *(squinting into the dark, then happy)*
Shed's too dark. [beat] Flashlight… [pause] hey. [beat] There you are. [pause] Found the ball. [beat] That's all I needed. ↘

*He looks at the long walk back to the house. Sighs. Looks at a cardboard box of old stuff by the shed door.*

**TAVIQUE** *(lazy, rationalizing, to himself)*
I'm not walking this *all* the way back to the hook. [beat] I'll just toss it in this box with the other stuff. [pause] *(tosses it — CLUNK)* I'll get it later.

**VESSA (from the yard, far — shouting, cupped hands)**
Tavique! [beat] That's the box for *junk!*

**TAVIQUE** *(shouting back, cheeky, already walking off with the ball)*
It's not junk! [beat] It's *later!* [beat] Same thing!

## 3. Mavi's afternoon

*EXT. SIDEWALK — DAY. Mavi is all energy. Anlin is dragged along, deadpan, in a shirt that is way too big. Their rhythm is the joke: Mavi fast and bouncy, Anlin slow and dry.*

**TAVIQUE** *(stops walking, staring, amused and confused)*
Mavi? [beat] Anlin? [pause] Hey — [beat] why are you dressed like that? [beat] Are those *paper poms?* ↗

**MAVI** *(bursting, shaking the poms, proud)*
Yes they're poms! [beat] I *made* them! [beat] We're doing *cheers.* [beat] That's the *WHOLE* plan.

**ANLIN** *(flat and deadpan — one tiny pom shake)*
[beat] Yay.

**MAVI** *(pointing at Anlin, bossy-sweet)*
You were *not* going to stay home. [beat] You're my cheer partner. [beat] Look at your shirt!

**ANLIN** *(dry, slow, looking down at it)*
You put this shirt on me.

**TAVIQUE** *(half laughing, curious)*
Cheers for *what?* [beat] Where are you even going? ↗

**MAVI** *(counting on her fingers, bouncing)*
To do cheers! [beat] I made the poms, [beat] I got the big shirts, [beat] and we're gonna stand there and *cheer.* [pause] That's the *fun* part.

**ANLIN** *(deadpan, to Tavique)*
That's *HER* fun part.

**TAVIQUE** *(pressing, a little louder)*
Yeah but cheer at *WHAT?* [beat] What game? ↗

**MAVI** *(thinking hard, eyes up, slower — guessing)*
Soccer… [beat] or football… [beat] maybe basketball? [pause] *(decides, bright)* Yeah. [beat] That's right. [beat] Basketball. [pause] *(smaller)* I think.

**TAVIQUE** *(disbelieving, a laugh in it)*
You don't even *know* what they're playing? ↗

**MAVI** *(breezy, totally unbothered)*
I don't *NEED* to know. [beat] We're not playing. [beat] We're the *cheerleaders.*

**ANLIN** *(tugging Mavi's sleeve, slow, done)*
Come on, let's go… [beat] so we can get this over with.

**TAVIQUE** *(stepping in front of them, more interested now)*
Okay but who's *playing* then? ↗

**MAVI** *(waving a pom vaguely, rambling)*
Whoever wants to put a team together I think. [beat] They said something about the winners *staying up* — [beat] whatever *THAT* means — [beat] and they said something about having *scorekeepers.*

*Tavique goes very still. His eyes change.*

**MAVI** *(oblivious, rolling on)*
Some other group of kids is coming. [beat] From the other side of town, I guess. [pause] They think they're like, [beat] the *best* or whatever.

**MAVI** *(chipper, fixing her pom)*
Anyway. [beat] We're gonna be the cheerleaders. [beat] That's the part that matters.

**TAVIQUE** *(slow, each word landing heavier — hooked)*
Hold on. [beat] Hold on. [pause] Winners stay. [beat] Scorekeepers. [pause] And kids who think they're the *BEST?* ↗

**MAVI** *(shrugging, not getting it at all)*
That's what they said. [beat] I don't know. [beat] It doesn't mean anything to *me.* [pause] We're gonna *cheer!*

**TAVIQUE** *(quieter, to himself, jaw set — competitive fire)*
Kids who think *THEY'RE* the best? ↘

**MAVI** *(impatient, already walking)*
Tavique. [beat] We're *leaving.* [beat] You coming to watch us or not? ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(distracted, already somewhere else in his head)*
I gotta go home first. [pause] I'll… [beat] I'll see you there.

## 4. The window

*EXT. MAIN ROAD — DAY. Tavique walking fast, talking himself up. Then the window stops him cold.*

**TAVIQUE** *(pacing, muttering, building)*
Winners stay up. [beat] Real scorekeepers. [beat] Kids from the other side who think they're the best. ↘

**TAVIQUE** *(a scoff that turns into a grin, fists up)*
Ha! [beat] I'm gonna show them. [pause] I'm gonna walk on that court and show them that *I'M* the best. ↗

*He stops mid-step. Slowly backs up two steps.*

**TAVIQUE** *(breathless, eyes huge)*
Whoa. [beat] Whoa, hold on. [pause] Look at the swap-shop window.

**TAVIQUE** *(hushed, almost reverent, hands on the glass)*
Those shoes. [beat] Sitting right there on that box. [pause] They look brand *new.* [beat] They're *SHINY.* ↗

*He looks down at his own feet. The toe of one sneaker is split open.*

**TAVIQUE** *(deflating, embarrassed, wiggling his toe through the hole)*
Now look at *mine.* [beat] These? [pause] These are the same shoes I mowed the yard in. [beat] The toe's coming *open.* ↘

**TAVIQUE** *(urgent, talking himself into it)*
I can't go out there in *THESE.* [beat] Not if they're keeping *score.* [pause] I *have* to have those.

**TAVIQUE** *(decided, grabbing the door handle)*
[beat] I'm going in.

## 5. The box

*INT. SWAP SHOP / HOME / SHOP — DAY. The Shopkeeper is calm, slow, a little knowing. He never rushes. Tavique rushes everything. That contrast is the scene.*

**TAVIQUE** *(breathless, pointing back at the window)*
Hey — [beat] those shoes in the window. [beat] The shiny ones sitting on that box. [pause] What size are they? [beat] What do you want for them? ↗

**SHOPKEEPER** *(unhurried, lifting them down)*
Try them on. [pause] I think the boy outgrew them. [beat] He was a little bigger than you. [pause] Traded them in for a game he *really* wanted.

**TAVIQUE** *(momentarily curious)*
A game? ↗

**SHOPKEEPER** *(a small knowing smile)*
That's what he said. [beat] Go on. [beat] See if they fit.

*Tavique yanks his old shoes off, pulls the shiny ones on. Stands. Wiggles. Looks down. Joy.*

**TAVIQUE** *(amazed, rising with each phrase)*
They fit. [beat] They fit *perfect.* [pause] They're my *size!* ↗

**SHOPKEEPER** *(dry, slow, with weight he doesn't explain)*
Then you're luckier than he was. [pause] Fill a box. [beat] Take a box.

**TAVIQUE** *(confused, head tilt)*
Fill a box? [beat] Take a box? [pause] What does that *mean?* ↗

**SHOPKEEPER** *(patient, counting it out on his fingers)*
You go home. [beat] You fill a box with stuff. [beat] You bring me the box. [beat] You take the shoes. [pause] That's the trade.

**TAVIQUE** *(already backing toward the door, pulling the shoes off)*
I don't have a box on me. [beat] I'll be right back!

**SHOPKEEPER** *(calm, calling after him, not looking up)*
They'll still be in the window. [pause] If you're *fast.*

**TAVIQUE** *(from the doorway, grinning)*
I'm *fast!*

*HE RUNS HOME — at the shed, the junk box.*

**TAVIQUE** *(frantic, grabbing, talking as fast as the scene allows — still every word clear)*
Box, box, box — [beat] this one. [beat] Okay. [beat] Fill it. [beat] Fill it. [pause] Cords. [beat] That broken controller. [beat] These old cars. [beat] This sock that isn't even a pair.

*He steps back. Looks. It's sad-looking.*

**TAVIQUE** *(frustrated, whiny)*
Come *on.* [beat] It's not enough. [beat] It still looks like junk. [pause] He said *FILL* a box.

**TAVIQUE** *(grabbing blindly, tossing things in)*
This — [beat] whatever this is — [beat] in. [beat] This here... [beat] and that there. [beat] In. [beat] What's on the hook —

*CLUNK. The old flashlight hits the box with the rest. He doesn't look. [pause] Then he does.*

**TAVIQUE** *(slowing to a stop, quiet)*
Okay. [beat] That's full. [beat] That's — [hold] wait.

**TAVIQUE** *(very quiet, realizing)*
That's Dad's flashlight. [pause] I threw Dad's flashlight in the junk box. ↘

**TAVIQUE** *(slow, uneasy — the temptation, said out loud)*
[pause] …It's the only thing in here that's worth anything. [pause] It's the only thing that'll get me those shoes.

**VESSA** *(appearing in the doorway, alarmed, then hurt)*
Tavique? [beat] What are you *doing?* [pause] That's *Dad's* flashlight. [beat] That's the one he *gave* you.

**TAVIQUE** *(not looking at her, defensive, low)*
I *know* what it is. [pause] But, I need the shoes for the game.

**VESSA** *(pleading, stepping closer, slower)*
It's the one he used in the *storm.* [beat] He said it was the *GOOD* one. [pause] He said take care of a light like that.

**TAVIQUE** *(rising, louder, desperate — convincing himself more than her)*
I can get another light, [beat] but the game is *TODAY!* [pause] I saw these shoes and… [beat] I *HAVE* to have them for this game.

**VESSA** *(small, disbelieving)*
[pause] You're *trading* it? ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(lifting the box, already moving, not meeting her eyes)*
I'm trading the whole box. [beat] I gotta go. [beat] Someone else might get them.

*[hold] on Vessa, alone in the doorway.*

*BACK TO THE SHOP*

**TAVIQUE** *(out of breath, thumping the box on the counter)*
I filled it. [beat] Here's the box. [beat] I want the shoes. [pause] They already fit.

**SHOPKEEPER** *(looks at the box, then at Tavique — slow, giving him a chance)*
Full box for the pair. [beat] That's the trade. [pause] You *sure?* ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(a tiny hesitation — then fast)*
[beat] I'm sure. [beat] Deal.

**SHOPKEEPER** *(slides the shoes across, pulls the box in)*
Alright. [beat] Shoes are yours. [pause] Box is mine.

**TAVIQUE** *(hopping, pulling them on, excited again)*
I have to put these on. [beat] I'm already *late!*

## 6. The stew hours

*EXT. SCHOOL COURT — DAY INTO LATE DAY. The fun is REAL — play it big and happy. Mavi and Anlin at the fence are the comic runner. The thunder comes late and far.*

**QUEN** *(jogging over, big grin)*
Tavique! [beat] You *came!* [pause] *(looks down)* And those are *NEW* shoes. [beat] We're short one. [beat] You in? ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(confident, cool, bouncing on his toes)*
I'm in. [pause] Who are those kids at the other end? ↗

**HALDEN** *(low, serious, sizing them up)*
The ones who think they're the best. [beat] Winners stay. [pause] Scorekeepers are writing on the *brick.*

**TAVIQUE** *(grin, eyes narrowing)*
Good. [beat] Let's *stay.*

*THEY PLAY*

**MAVI** *(full voice, arms up, poms shaking)*
Two, four, six, eight — [beat] who do we appreciate? ↗

**ANLIN** *(deadpan, arms at her sides)*
Appreciate *who?* [beat] We don't even know their names.

**MAVI** *(undeterred, sparkling)*
We don't *need* names! [beat] It's a *cheer!* [beat] Come on! [beat] Kick with me!

**ANLIN** *(flat)*
What kick?

**MAVI** *(kicking, joyful)*
This one! [beat] Kick! [beat] Kick! [beat] — go Tavique!

**ANLIN** *(flat, one tiny half-kick)*
[beat] Go Tavique.

**QUEN** *(shouting, fist pump)*
That's *GAME!* [beat] Winners stay! [pause] Tavique, that was *yours!*

**HALDEN** *(dry, satisfied smirk)*
They don't look like the best *anymore.*

*LATER — HE IS TIRED AND GLAD. Sun lower. Everyone sweaty and happy.*

**JAS** *(bouncing, breathless, admiring)*
Tavique you were on *fire!* [beat] I lost count of how many we won!

**QUEN** *(laughing, slapping Tavique's shoulder)*
Best shoes on the court, man. [beat] We stayed like *five* games.

**TAVIQUE** *(grinning, proud, winded)*
Told you. [pause] Somebody toss me that towel. [beat] I'm sweating through my shirt.

**HALDEN** *(tosses it, easy)*
Here. [beat] You earned it.

**MAVI** *(running up to the fence, hopeful)*
Tavique! [beat] Did you see us cheer? [beat] We did the kick one and *everything!* ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(towel on his head, half-apologetic, half-dismissive)*
Mavi. [beat] We were busy playing the game. [beat] I wasn't watching the fence.

**MAVI** *(little shrug, still proud)*
We were *loud* though.

**ANLIN** *(deadpan, rubbing her arm)*
*You* were loud. [beat] I got *dragged.*

*[pause] Thunder. Far. [pause] Then closer. Everyone looks up.*

**JAS** *(nervous, glancing at the sky)*
Was that thunder? [pause] *(it rumbles again)* That *was* thunder.

**MAVI** *(pointing, worried, faster)*
Look at the sky. [beat] Those clouds on the horizon are *DARK.* [beat] We'd better head home.

**TAVIQUE** *(looking down at his shoes first — that's the tell)*
Yeah. [beat] Don't want to ruin my shoes in the rain. [beat] These are *new.*

**TAVIQUE** *(waving everyone on, leader energy)*
Come to my house, guys! [beat] It's closer than anybody else's. [pause] We can beat the storm!

## 7. Closer

*EXT. STREET — DUSK. Rain starting, puddles. Still light enough to see. Tavique cares about one thing: the shoes. It's funny.*

**JAS** *(yelping, hands over his head)*
Those are *real* drops! [beat] *Big* ones!

**TAVIQUE** *(tiptoeing, hopping sideways around a puddle)*
Walk *around* the puddles! [beat] I'm *not* getting these shoes wet. [pause] My house is the next street!

**MAVI** *(jogging beside him, completely unbothered by rain)*
I still want to know if you saw the kick cheer.

**TAVIQUE** *(mid-hop, each word a separate jump)*
Can't right now… [beat] trying… [beat] to…. [beat] avoid…. [beat] puddles.

**HALDEN** *(dry, rain dripping off his hair)*
Your house better have a roof. [beat] That's all I care about.

**TAVIQUE** *(chuckles, over his shoulder)*
Halden… [beat] It's got a *roof.*

## 8. The hook

*INT. TAVIQUE'S HOUSE — DUSK INTO NIGHT. First half: dim but visible, relieved, cozy. Second half: the lights die and the room goes black. Real fear — they register it with their bodies (flinching, huddling), not with a speech.*

**TAVIQUE** *(bursting in, bossy host)*
Everybody in! [beat] Shoes by the door — [beat] not in the wet. [pause] *(surprised)* Vessa? [beat] You're already home? ↗

**VESSA** *(on the couch, knees up, a little uneasy)*
I've been here. [beat] The storm got loud.

**JAS** *(looking around, reassuring himself)*
At least we can still *see.*

**MAVI** *(bumping a chair, laughing)*
Enough to not walk into the chair.

**ANLIN** *(deadpan, rubbing her shin)*
Speak for *yourself.*

*NIGHT. FLASH — lightning fills the windows. Everyone flinches.*

**JAS** *(startled, high)*
The windows just lit up! [beat] That was *lightning!*

*The lights flicker. [beat] Then die. Black.*

**VESSA** *(scared, quiet, slow)*
The lights went out. [pause] It's night. [beat] The *whole* house is dark.

**TAVIQUE** *(brave voice, taking charge, moving)*
It's okay! [beat] The flashlight's on the hook. [beat] I just have to —

*His hand pats the wall. Finds the hook. Nothing on it. [hold]*

**TAVIQUE** *(voice falling apart, slow)*
It's not here. [pause] It's… [beat] oh no… ↘

**VESSA** *(soft, not mean — she knew)*
[pause] I know.

**TAVIQUE** *(grasping, faster, hopeful)*
Hold on. [beat] The drawer. [beat] Dad's new one should be — [beat] Vessa, is the new one in the drawer? ↗

**VESSA** *(gentle, sorry to say it)*
Dad took that one with him. [pause] He's not here.

*He stares at the empty hook in the dark. He remembers the box. [hold]*

**TAVIQUE** *(barely above a whisper)*
Mine. [pause] The old one. [pause] The one I put in the box. ↘

## 9. Own it

*INT. HOUSE — CONTINUOUS. Pitch dark. Physical comedy first — then it turns honest. Let the turn be slow.*

*THUD.*

**TAVIQUE** *(pained, then outraged)*
Ow — [beat] that's the chair! [beat] Is that *ALWAYS* right here? ↗

**MAVI** *(worried, reaching out blindly)*
I can't see you! [beat] What did you hit?

**TAVIQUE** *(through gritted teeth, shuffling)*
The *chair!* [pause] I'm going to the kitchen. [beat] Don't anybody move.

*CRASH — a plastic cup clatters, rolls.*

**TAVIQUE** *(wincing, each disaster a new beat)*
Whoa… [beat] that was a cup. [beat] I knocked a cup off the counter. [beat] Oh man, [beat] it's rolling away. [pause] *Ouch!* [beat] I just stepped on something pointy and small —

**TAVIQUE** *(hops, yelps)*
*OW!* [beat] My *TOE.* [beat] I stubbed my toe on the table leg.

**ANLIN** *(calm, practical, from the dark)*
Use your tablet light!

**TAVIQUE** *(exasperated, near tears of frustration)*
My tablet's in the *other* room! [beat] I think! [pause] I can't even find the *doorway* and I walk through it *every day!*

**JAS** *(small voice)*
I can't see my own feet.

**HALDEN** *(dry)*
I can *hear* your feet. [beat] And his toe.

*[hold]. Tavique sinks down against the counter. The joking stops.*

**TAVIQUE** *(lamenting — slow, heavy, honest)*
I put it in the box. [pause] I put Dad's flashlight in the junk box at the shop. [pause] It's *gone.* ↘

**VESSA** *(quiet — not an accusation, a real question)*
[pause] Why'd you do that?

**TAVIQUE** *(ashamed, small)*
For the shoes. [pause] So I could show them I was the best. ↘

**MAVI** *(soft, a little lost)*
We just wait in the dark then? ↗

**VESSA** *(hopeful, leaning toward his voice)*
Can you go get it? [beat] From the shop? ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(looking at the black window, flat)*
The shop's closed. [beat] It's night.

**ANLIN** *(matter-of-fact)*
So we sit here till morning.

**TAVIQUE** *(firm, a promise to himself — the first bit of owning it)*
First thing. [beat] I'll be there when they unlock. [pause] I don't have the light tonight. ↘

## 10. No reverse

*INT. SWAP SHOP — FIRST THING IN THE MORNING. Old shoes on his feet. Shiny pair in his hands, held out like an offering. Vessa beside him. The Shopkeeper is kind but it doesn't change anything.*

**TAVIQUE** *(breathless, rushing it all out, hopeful)*

## 11. What he kept

*INT. HOUSE — THAT SAME MORNING. Daylight. The empty hook in plain view. Tavique still holding the shoes. Quiet sibling scene — then a joke, then a chase. The warmth comes back.*

**VESSA** *(gentle, sitting on the arm of the couch)*
You can put those down now.

**TAVIQUE** *(not moving, staring at the hook)*
In a minute.

**VESSA** *(soft, matter-of-fact)*
They're the same shoes he already said no to.

**TAVIQUE** *(quiet)*
I heard him.

**VESSA** *(curious, kind)*
Then why are you still holding them? ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(slow, the real answer)*
[pause] If I put them down [beat] it means that's it.

**VESSA** *(gently)*
It already is.

**TAVIQUE** *(sets them on the chair — a long exhale)*
[pause] Fine.

**VESSA**
You gonna wear them? ↗

**TAVIQUE** *(shakes his head)*
Not right now.

**VESSA** *(a tiny teasing lift)*
Your toe's still open. [beat] I heard it the whole way back.

**TAVIQUE** *(looking at the hook, serious)*
[pause] Dad's going to look at the hook.

**VESSA** *(quiet)*
I know.

**TAVIQUE** *(steady, owning it — this is his character: fast and honest)*
I'll tell him *before* he looks. [pause] I traded it. [beat] That was *me.* ↘

**VESSA** *(warm, trying to cheer him up)*
You *were* the best one out there yesterday.

**TAVIQUE** *(small smile, honest)*
For a while.

**VESSA** *(firm, kind)*

**TAVIQUE** *(looking at the hook one more time)*
[pause] The light counted too. ↘

*[hold]. Then Vessa's eyes get mischievous.*

**VESSA** *(sly)*

Next time the lights go out, [beat] you can run into the chair [beat] in *style.*

In the *shiny* ones!

## 12. Cutaway

*CUTAWAY — A DIFFERENT LOOK. Paper cut-out Bible-parallel style (PAR-STYLE-001). Vessa narrates as storyteller — warm, slower than anywhere else in the episode, the way you tell a story at bedtime. She does both brothers' voices. Esau: bigger, gruffer, dramatic. Jacob: smooth, quiet, a little too calm.*

**VESSA** *(storyteller voice, hushed, drawing you in)*
This isn't our street. [pause] This is *older.* [pause] There's dust, [beat] and a tent, [beat] and a pot on the fire.

**VESSA** *(slow, weary tone for Esau)*
A man comes in from the field. [beat] His name is Esau. [pause] He's been out there *all* day. [beat] You can hear how tired his feet are.

**VESSA**
And Esau goes —

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(gruff, sniffing the air, dramatic)*
I can *smell* that from outside. [beat] What *is* that? ↗

**VESSA**
And his brother Jacob's got the pot, [beat] and he says —

**VESSA (as JACOB)** *(smooth, easy, stirring)*
Stew. [beat] Red stew. [pause] It's been on since morning.

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(over-the-top, clutching his stomach)*
Give me some. [beat] I'm *starving.* [pause] I was in that field till I couldn't see straight. [beat] I think I might *die.*

**VESSA**
And Jacob says —

**VESSA (as JACOB)** *(slow, sly, careful — choosing words)*
I'll give you the bowl. [pause] You give me… [beat] what you get… [beat] you know. [pause] What you get for being the *oldest.*

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(scoffing, waving it off)*
*That?* [beat] What good is that if I fall over right here? ↗

**VESSA** *(dropping out of character, a little wry aside to us)*
[pause] He wasn't dying. [beat] He was just *that* hungry.

**VESSA**
Then Jacob says —

**VESSA (as JACOB)** *(quiet, serious)*
Then swear it. [beat] That, for the stew. [pause] Right now.

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(impatient, careless — tossing it away)*
Fine. [beat] I swear. [beat] Give me the bowl.

**VESSA**
And Jacob hands it over, [beat] and he goes —

**VESSA (as JACOB)** *(pleasant, generous-sounding)*
Here. [beat] Bread too. [pause] Don't say I sent you away empty.

**VESSA** *(slow, each action its own beat)*
And Esau eats. [beat] He drinks. [beat] And he goes —

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(satisfied sigh, leaning back)*
That's better. [pause] That's — [beat] that's all I needed. ↘

**VESSA** *(slower still, flat, letting it land)*
He ate. [beat] He drank. [beat] He stood up. [beat] He wiped his mouth. [pause] And he left.

*LATER*

**VESSA** *(quieter, the tone shifting)*
And later [beat] Esau comes back, [beat] and he goes —

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(desperate, pleading)*
Wait. [beat] That was *mine.* [pause] I want it back. [beat] I'll give you something else. [beat] *Anything* else. ↗

**VESSA (as JACOB)** *(calm, final)*
[pause] You already gave it. [beat] You swore.

**VESSA (as ESAU)** *(small, broken)*
[pause] I was hungry. ↘

**VESSA** *(soft, the last line of the episode — slowest line in the show)*
[pause] He was sorry. [hold] It was already gone.

*[hold] — fade.*
